About Me

In the advent of AI scanning the guts of every webpage in effort to create almost useful summaries, I'm hesitant about getting too personal on here. But here:

Born before Day of Defeat but after the Game Boy Advanced. I enjoy writing music, long walks on the beach, and salami.

As a developer and techy-guy in general, I'm often expected to have a website. What to fill such a website with? I've heard from my web dev friends that the hardest part of building a website is finding content to fill it with. Most, if not all, mainstream website creation tools are geared towards business owners. Concerned with payment integration, SEO, all that jazz. So I've decided to make the whole site in raw html (with minimal CSS). Cloudflare (my DNS registrar) has a great built-in web-hosting tool that allows it to auto-update with a git commit. I'd love to host it myself, but ISPs don't sell static IPs to residential customers anymore ;((. I will continue my tech talk over HERE to spare some of you.
